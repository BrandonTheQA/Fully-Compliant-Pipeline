## Feature Details

### Key Capabilities
- Gift card purchase (fixed amounts, custom amounts, multiple cards)
- Gift card redemption during checkout
- Balance management and inquiry
- Email delivery to recipients
- Expiration and validity management
- Guest purchase support
- Admin management and analytics
- Mobile-optimized experience

### Business Value Highlights
- **Revenue Growth**: 10-15% increase in overall revenue
- **Customer Acquisition**: 30-40% of gift card recipients are new customers
- **Higher Transaction Values**: 15-25% increase in average transaction value
- **Cash Flow**: Upfront payments improve working capital
- **Cart Abandonment Reduction**: 8-12% reduction through convenient gifting option
- **Annual Impact**: ~$660,000 (including new customer acquisition value)

### Industry Research
- 72% of consumers purchase gift cards annually
- Gift card sales increase overall revenue by 10-15% annually
- 30-40% of gift card recipients are new customers
- Gift cards have 20-30% higher redemption rates than traditional promotional codes
- Average gift card transaction value is 15-25% higher than regular purchases
- Gift card recipients spend 20-40% more than the card value on average
- 25-35% of gift card recipients become repeat customers
